# Demo Boundary

## Public claim

Booking Leakage is an **interactive synthetic demonstration** of workflow-control and revalidation behavior.

## It may be described as

- a working interactive demo;
- a synthetic private-aviation workflow scenario;
- a deterministic booking-control prototype;
- applied Clay Waterson systems work.

## It must not be described as

- a live brokerage booking engine;
- a production SeshCloud deployment;
- a verified client outcome;
- an autonomous booking or payment system;
- connected to live operator, client or payment systems.

## Data rule

Use synthetic or deliberately sanitized records only.
